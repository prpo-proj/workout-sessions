# Workout Tracker: Workout Sessions

Workout Tracker helps lifters log their strength training and review past workouts.

The Workout Sessions service lets a lifter start a workout, add exercises from the catalogue, and record sets as they train. They can also review completed workouts in their history.

## Planned MVP

- Start an empty workout and add exercises from the catalogue.
- Record sets with repetitions and weight.
- Finish a workout and review it in history.

## Build, run, and test

The service is in the design stage. Build, run, and test instructions will be added when the first runnable version is available.
